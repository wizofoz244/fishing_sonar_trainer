GARMIN GPSMAP® SIMULATOR GUIDE

Welcome to the marine acoustics lab! Here is how to navigate and use the simulator:

🐟 1. Add & Drag Targets

Click anywhere in the lake to spawn fish, or drag items (Bass, Port/Stbd fish, Boulders, Timber, Jig) directly from the bottom dock into the water.

🎯 2. Cross-Screen Lock

Click any fish, boulder, or timber badge on any view to illuminate a laser locator beacon and lock tactical tracking across all 4 sonars.

📡 3. 4 Sonar Technologies

Compare LiveScope (real-time video), SideVü (shadows & keel pinch), ClearVü (razor silhouettes), and 2D CHIRP (arches).

⚙️ 4. Helm & Drawers

Adjust speed, depth, and gain at the bottom. Use the top drawer buttons (Angles, Targets, Lakebed) for acoustic geometry and bathymetry.

💡 Pro-Tip:
Toggle 🎯 Correlate to display letter badges ([A1], [B1], etc.) that connect objects in the lake directly to their acoustic shapes on each sounder.
